## tooling

- `tools/check_doc_symbols.py` only knows the KN5000 disassembly (it requires a `v10/` tree), so it
  reports every KN7000 name as unknown. The SP7001 pages were checked by hand against
  `kn7000_disassembly`'s `*.sym` files and `src/sp7001/*.s` (48 of 51 backticked names found; the
  other three are a placeholder, a repository name and a MAME device name). Teach the checker a
  KN7000 mode that reads `kn7000_disassembly/*.sym` and the `.equ` lines of `src/**/*.s`.

## owner: publish

- `kn7000_disassembly` is unpublished; the SP7001 page cites its `notes/sp7001-build-diff.md`
  and its make targets. Publishing it (without `baserom/`, which is git-ignored) would make
  those citations checkable.
- Push this repository so the pages go live.

## upstream MAME (owner decision)

- `src/mame/matsushita/kn7000.cpp` says of the `sp7001` set: "Its SMCK checksum predates the final
  payload and does not match". The table half of that is now exact: `SMCKTB1/2.INF` match the table
  without the plug-in and with segments 68–70 reading `83` instead of `80`, and the two table disks
  are byte-identical to the declared ROMs. A later PR could say so in the comment, or not bother.
